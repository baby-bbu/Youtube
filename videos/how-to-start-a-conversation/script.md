# The rundown on starting a conversation

Idea: Small talk isn't about being interesting, it's about asking: notice, ask an open question, follow up.

Target: about 25 seconds, about 79 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-start-a-conversation`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. Small talk isn't about being interesting.
2. Why do conversations die after hello?
3. Never run out of things to say again.

## Script
Small talk isn't about being interesting.

Most people think they need a clever opener.

But all you really need is a question.

Step one. Notice something around you. "Nice jacket, where's it from?"

Step two. Ask an open question. One that can't be answered with yes or no.

Step three. Follow up. Pick one thing they said and ask more about it.

People like feeling listened to, so the conversation runs itself.

That's the rundown. Be curious, not clever.
