# The rundown on how to stop overthinking

Idea: Overthinking is a loop. Get it out of your head, do or drop it, and give worries a scheduled slot.

Target: about 35 seconds, about 100 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-stop-overthinking`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. Your brain isn't broken. It's stuck on a loop.
2. Why can't you stop replaying everything?
3. Stop overthinking in three steps.

## Script
Your brain isn't broken. It's just stuck on a loop.

Thinking harder won't get you out of it.

Step one. Get it out of your head. Say it out loud or write it down.

Step two. Ask, can I do something about this today? If yes, do the next tiny step. If no, let it go for now.

Step three. Set a worry time. Ten minutes later in the day. If the thought comes back, tell it to wait.

Do all three, and the loop loses its grip.

That's the rundown. Write it down, then do it or drop it.
