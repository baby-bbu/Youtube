# The rundown on saving money

Idea: Saving what's left never works. Pay yourself first, wait before buying, and plug small leaks. (General tips only, no promises.)

Target: about 25 seconds, about 81 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-save-money`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. You don't have a money problem. You have a leak problem.
2. Why is there never anything left to save?

## Script
You don't have a money problem. You have a leak problem.

Most people try to save whatever is left at the end of the month. There's never anything left.

Step one. Pay yourself first. Move a small amount to savings the day you get paid.

Step two. Wait twenty four hours before buying anything you don't need.

Step three. Check your subscriptions. Cancel the ones you forgot about.

Small leaks add up fast.

That's the rundown. Save first, spend what's left.
