# The rundown on speaking in public

Idea: Nervous speakers rush. Slow down, pause instead of saying um, and make just one point.

Target: about 25 seconds, about 80 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-speak-in-public`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. Nervous speakers talk too fast.
2. Why do you freeze when everyone's watching?
3. Speak in public without panicking.

## Script
Nervous speakers talk too fast.

Most people think they need to sound impressive.

But the audience just wants to understand you.

Step one. Slow down. Way slower than feels natural.

Step two. Pause instead of saying um. A silence feels longer to you than it does to them.

Step three. Make one point. Say it, show why it matters, and stop.

Do all three, and you'll sound calm even if you're not.

That's the rundown. Slow down, pause, one point.
