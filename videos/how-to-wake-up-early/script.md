# The rundown on waking up without hitting snooze

Idea: Make getting up easier by design: alarm across the room, light right away, one thing to look forward to.

Target: about 30 seconds, about 88 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-wake-up-early`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. The snooze button is lying to you.
2. Why is it so hard to get out of bed?
3. Stop hitting snooze with this.

## Script
The snooze button is lying to you.

You think you need willpower in the morning.

But you just need to make getting up easier.

Step one. Put your alarm across the room. You have to stand up to turn it off.

Step two. Get light right away. Open the curtains or turn on a lamp.

Step three. Have one thing to look forward to. Even just a good coffee.

Do all three, and mornings get a lot less painful.

That's the rundown. Make getting up the easy option.
