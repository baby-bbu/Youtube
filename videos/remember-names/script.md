# The rundown on remembering names

Target: about 45 seconds, about 95 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. You forget names the second you hear them.
2. Why do you forget a name in five seconds?
3. Stop forgetting names. Here's the trick.

## Script
You forget names the second you hear them.

Most people think they're just bad with names.

But the truth is, the name never made it into your memory in the first place.

Step one. Say it back right away.

"Nice to meet you, Sam." That one repeat makes you actually listen.

Step two. Picture it.

Link the name to something you can see, like Sam holding a sandwich.

Step three. Use it twice in the first minute.

Then say it once more when you leave.

Do all three, and you'll blank on names a lot less.

Because now it's stored, not just heard.

That's the rundown. Next time someone says their name, catch it.
