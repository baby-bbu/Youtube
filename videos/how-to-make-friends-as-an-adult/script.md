# The rundown on making friends as an adult

Idea: Adult friendships take repetition: show up again, be the one who invites, follow up within a week.

Target: about 30 seconds, about 89 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-make-friends-as-an-adult`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. Making friends as an adult isn't hard. It's just not automatic.
2. Why is it so hard to make friends now?
3. Make friends as an adult in three steps.

## Script
Making friends as an adult isn't hard. It's just not automatic.

In school you saw the same people every day. Now you have to create that yourself.

Step one. Keep showing up to the same place. A class, a gym, a club. Familiar faces turn into friends.

Step two. Be the one who invites. Keep it small. Coffee, a walk, anything.

Step three. Follow up within a week. That's the step most people skip.

Do all three, and acquaintances become friends.

That's the rundown. Show up, invite, follow up.
