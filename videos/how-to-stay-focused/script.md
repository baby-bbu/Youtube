# The rundown on how to stay focused

Idea: Nobody focuses for hours. Work in short sprints: one task, a 25 minute timer, a real break.

Target: about 25 seconds, about 71 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-stay-focused`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. You can't focus for hours. Nobody can.
2. Why does your focus keep breaking?
3. Stay focused with this simple trick.

## Script
You can't focus for hours. Nobody can.

Most people try to power through, and end up distracted anyway.

Step one. Pick one task. Just one.

Step two. Set a timer for twenty five minutes and work on only that.

Step three. Take a five minute break. Away from your screen.

After four rounds, take a longer break.

Short sprints beat long struggles.

That's the rundown. One task, one timer, then rest.
