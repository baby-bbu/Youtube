# The rundown on how to say no

Idea: Saying no kindly: pause first, keep it short and warm, don't over-explain.

Target: about 25 seconds, about 79 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-say-no`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. Saying no doesn't make you rude.
2. Why can't you ever say no?
3. Say no without feeling guilty.

## Script
Saying no doesn't make you rude.

Most people say yes because they're scared of letting someone down.

Step one. Pause. You don't have to answer right away. Say, let me check and get back to you.

Step two. Say no kindly and keep it short. "I can't make it, but thanks for asking."

Step three. Don't over-explain. Long excuses just invite arguments.

A clear, kind no is easier on everyone.

That's the rundown. Pause, be kind, keep it short.
