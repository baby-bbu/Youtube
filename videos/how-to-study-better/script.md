# The rundown on how to study better

Idea: Re-reading feels like studying but isn't. Pull answers out of your head instead (active recall, then spacing).

Target: about 25 seconds, about 81 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine). Not made yet: the scenes (`shots.json`)
still need to be written before running `python3 tools/make.py how-to-study-better`.

## Hook options (the first is used)
1. Re-reading your notes is a waste of time.
2. Why do you forget everything after you study?
3. Stop studying like this. Do this instead.

## Script
Re-reading your notes is a waste of time.

It feels like studying, but your brain is just recognizing the words.

Real learning happens when you have to pull the answer out.

Step one. Close the book. Write down everything you remember.

Step two. Check what you missed. Those gaps are exactly what to study next.

Step three. Do it again tomorrow. Spacing it out beats cramming.

Do all three, and the stuff actually sticks.

That's the rundown. Stop re-reading. Start recalling.
