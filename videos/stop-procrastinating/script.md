# The rundown on how to stop procrastinating

Target: about 35 seconds, about 100 spoken words. Read it at a normal pace, no rush.
Record it, drop the file in this folder (any name, .m4a is fine), then run
`python3 tools/make.py stop-procrastinating`.

## Script
You're not lazy. You just can't start.

Most people think they need motivation first.

But it works the other way around. Starting is what creates the motivation.

Step one. Make it tiny.

Don't write the essay. Just write one sentence.

Step two. Set a two minute timer.

Promise yourself you can quit when it rings.

Step three. Put your phone in another room.

Out of sight, out of mind.

Do all three, and starting gets a lot easier.

Because the hardest part is the first two minutes.

That's the rundown. Next time you're stalling, start small.
