# Rundown Guy: Brand Sheet

Decided 2026-10-11.

## Identity
- **Channel name:** Rundown Guy
- **Handle:** @rundownguy (YouTube page returned 404 when checked, so it was probably
  free; TikTok and Instagram were NOT confirmed. Claim all handles before posting.)
- **Tagline:** "the quick rundown on anything." (placeholder copy, easy to change)
- **Channel description (About):**
  > Rundown Guy here. You've got better things to do than watch a 20-minute video, so
  > I break things down fast. New skill, quick rundown, done.
- **Niche:** short-form how-to guides: a simple rundown of how to get good at, or do,
  something. Vertical 9:16, aimed at about 30 seconds.
- **Narrator:** the stickman is "the guy". He is the Rundown Guy.

## Colours
| Name   | Hex       | Use                                   |
|--------|-----------|----------------------------------------|
| Yellow | `#FFD400` | Primary brand colour, profile picture, titles |
| Black  | `#0F0F0F` | Backgrounds (banner), text on yellow  |
| Ink    | `#111111` | Character outlines and body           |
| White  | `#FFFFFF` | Face, tagline, captions               |

## Type
- **Titles / banner:** Inter, weight 900 (Black); tagline Inter 600. Inter is open
  licence (OFL).
- **Captions:** rounded, bold, white text with a thick black outline, one to three
  words at a time. Specific font NOT yet chosen; pick a rounded OFL font and record
  it here.

## Character (see `character/`)
- Big round white head, thick dark outline.
- Black **trucker cap worn backwards** (button on top, snapback strap tab at the
  front, no brim visible from the front).
- Flat black **shades**; they stay on.
- **Mouth:** white interior with a black outline, no fixed side. It moves left, centre
  or right as the face "turns", and changes shape per word. Shapes: big, small, teeth,
  closed (flat line with end ticks), oh.
- **Body:** short, thick, black, no outline, no feet; limbs end in rounded tips.
- On dark or busy backgrounds the character gets a white sticker outline.
- This is the character's own design. It borrows animation techniques from reference
  channels but deliberately does not copy another channel's character.
