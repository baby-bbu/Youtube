# Rundown Guy Renderer Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build the Remotion project that turns a hand-written `timeline.json` plus a voice file into a finished 1080x1920 video of the animated Rundown Guy, with captions, backgrounds, camera moves and slide transitions.

**Architecture:** Pure, unit-tested functions (timeline parsing, character motion, caption grouping, camera and transition maths, loudness) do all the decisions; thin React components only draw their output. The character is drawn from the SVG parts in `brand/character/rundown-guy.svg`. Everything random is derived from `timeline.seed`, so a re-render is identical.

**Tech Stack:** Node 22, TypeScript, Remotion 4.0.534 (all `remotion` and `@remotion/*` packages pinned to this exact version), React, Vitest (unit tests), ffmpeg/ffprobe (checks and fixtures), Python 3 stdlib (fixture generator only).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-11-stickman-video-pipeline-design.md` (sections 5, 9, 10; brand values in `brand/brand.md`). This is Plan 1 of 2. Plan 2 (audio prep, transcription, align, images, `make.py`, checks) is written after this one is reviewed.

## Global Constraints

- Output: 1080x1920 (9:16), 30 fps. Preview: 540x960 at 15 fps.
- All `remotion` and `@remotion/*` packages use the same exact version `4.0.534`.
- Brand colours: yellow `#FFD400`, black `#0F0F0F`, ink `#111111`, white `#FFFFFF`.
- Face turn: shades and mouth slide together by at most ±28 px; head tilt at most ±10 degrees; the cap stays on the head.
- Exactly six mouth shapes: `closed`, `small`, `oh`, `teeth`, `big`, `wide` (white interior, black outline).
- Character: big white head, backwards black trucker cap, shades always on, short thick black body, no feet. A white sticker outline is always used over images.
- Captions: one to three words at a time, lower third, rounded bold white with a thick black outline, pop-in scale per word. The font is one constant, `CAPTION_FONT`, so it can be changed later.
- Transitions: horizontal slide is the default; named types are `slide-left`, `slide-right`, `cut`, `zoom`, `whip`.
- Deterministic: the same `timeline.json` (including `seed`) must produce identical frames.
- No AI-generated images. A shot with no `image` shows a title card (label on a plain background).
- `timeline.json` is plain JSON in the format in spec section 5; it may be hand-edited.

## Review Focus

Failure modes the spec implies but a happy-path test would miss, most likely first. Each has a test in the task that owns the code.

1. A shot shorter than its transition (e.g. a 0.2 s shot) must clamp the transition, not overlap or crash. (Task 7)
2. A shot with no words (music-only or silent beat): mouth stays `closed`, no caption, no crash. (Tasks 4 and 6)
3. A very long word must shrink to fit the caption width instead of overflowing the frame. (Task 6)
4. A gap between shots, or audio before the first shot, must hold the previous shot on screen, never go black. (Task 2)
5. Silent or empty audio must give zero loudness and no `NaN`, and the mouth stays `closed`. (Task 5)
6. A shot with no `image` must render a title card, not throw. (Task 7)

### Task 4: Character motion

**Files:**
- Create: `remotion/src/Guy/motion.ts`
- Test: `remotion/src/Guy/motion.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `Timeline`, `Shot`, `GuyPose`, `MOUTHS` (Task 2); `shotAt`, `wordAt` (Task 2).
- Produces: `BEAT_SECONDS = 0.35`; `beatRandom(seed: number, beat: number, salt: number): number` (pure, in `[0,1)`); `guyPose(tl: Timeline, t: number, loudness: number): GuyPose`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write failing tests** (load the sample timeline with `parseTimeline`):
  - `beatRandom(1234, 5, 1)` equals itself on a second call and differs from `beatRandom(1235, 5, 1)`.
  - Determinism: `guyPose(tl, 3.2, 0.5)` deep-equals a second identical call.
  - Bounds: for every `t` from 0 to `tl.duration` step 0.05 and loudness 0.5, `Math.abs(faceX) <= 28` and `Math.abs(tilt) <= 10`.
  - Both sides (the mouth must not stick to one side): over the same samples, at least 10% have `faceX < -10` and at least 10% have `faceX > 10`.
  - Position: a time inside a `left` shot gives `x < 0`; inside a `right` shot gives `x > 0`; inside a `centre` shot `Math.abs(x) < 0.1`.
  - Mouth is always one of `MOUTHS`.
  - Silence: at a time with no active word, `mouth === 'closed'`. For a copy of the timeline whose shots all have `words: []`, every sampled pose has `mouth === 'closed'` (review focus 2).
  - Loudness gating: with an active word, loudness 0.05 gives `'small'`; loudness 0.9 gives `'big'` or `'wide'`.
  - Emphasis: the mean `bob` over samples inside emphasis words is greater than the mean `bob` inside non-emphasis words.
- [ ] **Step 2: Run to see them fail.**
- [ ] **Step 3: Implement** `beatRandom` as a stateless hash of `(seed, beat, salt)` (for example a mulberry32 step seeded by a mix of the three). Implement `guyPose` with these decisions:
  - `beat = floor(t / BEAT_SECONDS)`; the target `faceX` for a beat is `(beatRandom(seed, beat, 1) * 2 - 1) * 28`; `faceX` eases from the previous beat's target to this one over the first 0.12 s of the beat (smoothstep), giving the stepped, snappy feel.
  - `tilt = clamp(faceX / 28 * 8 + (beatRandom(seed, beat, 2) - 0.5) * 4, -10, 10)`.
  - `bob`: 14 px when the active word has `emphasis`, otherwise 4 px times `abs(sin)` over the beat.
  - `mouth`: `'closed'` when `wordAt` is `null`; else `'small'` if `loudness < 0.15`; `'big'` or `'wide'` (chosen by `beatRandom(..., 3)`) if `loudness >= 0.6`; otherwise one of `small`, `oh`, `teeth`, `big` chosen by `beatRandom(..., 4)`.
  - `x`: `-0.45`, `0`, `0.45` for `left`, `centre`, `right` of the current shot, plus a sway of at most 0.02 over time.
  - Arm angles come from the shot's `character.action` (`idle`: small constant sway; `point`: right arm raised about 40; `wave`: right arm oscillating with `t`; `shrug` and `bounce` raise both arms briefly on beats). Exact angles are free as long as they stay within ±60.
- [ ] **Step 4: Run to see them pass.**
